## Images

The four photographs in `examples/photos/` were generated with an image model
for this repository. They are not stock, not scraped, and contain no
identifiable person, place or mark. They are MIT like the rest of the repo, so
you can ship them in a fork or replace them without asking.

The six texture tiles in `tokens/assets/` are generated by
`tokens/make-textures.py` from a fixed random seed. Delete them and run that
script and you get the same six files back.
